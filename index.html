FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is your minimum order quantity (MOQ)?
Our standard MOQ is 100 pairs per design. For some products, we accept trial orders as low as 10 pairs. Contact us for details.
Can I customize the design, material, and packaging?
Absolutely. We offer full OEM/ODM — you can customize material (cotton, wool, polyester, blends), colors, logo placement (embroidery, jacquard, print), size, and packaging.
How long does sampling and production take?
Samples are ready in 5-7 days. Mass production typically takes 15-25 days depending on order quantity and complexity.
Do you provide free samples?
We provide free design service. Sample socks are charged at production cost, but the sample fee is refundable with your first bulk order.
What shipping methods do you offer?
We ship via air freight, sea freight, and express (DHL, FedEx, UPS). We also support Amazon FBA prep and direct-to-warehouse delivery.
