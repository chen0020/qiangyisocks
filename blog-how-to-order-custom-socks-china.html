How to Order Custom Socks from China: A Complete Buyer's Guide

Published September 3, 2026  ·  8 min read  ·  By Qiangyi Socks Team

Sourcing custom socks from China can save you 40–60% compared to domestic manufacturing — but only if you know what to ask, what to check, and what pitfalls to avoid. This guide walks you through the entire process, from finding a supplier to receiving your first bulk shipment.

Step 1: Define Your Product Before You Contact Anyone

Before reaching out to a factory, have clear answers to these four questions:

  • Sock type: Running, football, basketball, yoga, casual, kids?
  • Material: Cotton, polyester, nylon, merino wool, bamboo, or a blend?
  • Customization level: Logo only, full custom pattern, or custom silhouette?
  • Target quantity: How many pairs for the first order?

Having this ready means you'll get accurate quotes faster and avoid back-and-forth that wastes weeks.

Step 2: Understand MOQ — and Why It Matters

MOQ (Minimum Order Quantity) is the smallest number of units a factory will produce per style. For custom socks in China, MOQ typically ranges from 100 to 500 pairs per style depending on the supplier and complexity.

💡 Tip: If you're testing a new design, look for suppliers with MOQ of 100 pairs. This lets you validate the market before committing to a large run. At Qiangyi Socks, our MOQ is 100 pairs per style.

Lower MOQ usually means slightly higher per-unit cost, but it dramatically reduces your inventory risk on new products.

Step 3: OEM vs ODM — Which Is Right for You?

These two terms come up constantly in B2B sourcing:

  • OEM (Original Equipment Manufacturer): You provide the design — colors, patterns, logo placement, material spec — and the factory produces to your exact requirements. Best for brands with a clear design vision.
  • ODM (Original Design Manufacturer): The factory offers existing designs or templates that you can modify and brand as your own. Best for buyers who want speed and don't have an in-house design team.

Most buyers start with ODM to move quickly, then shift to OEM once they know what sells.

Step 4: Request Samples Before Any Bulk Order

Never skip the sample stage. A professional supplier will offer pre-production samples (usually 2–5 pairs) so you can verify:

  • Color accuracy vs. your Pantone reference
  • Knit quality and thickness
  • Logo placement and print quality
  • Sizing consistency
  • Packaging and labeling

Standard sample lead time from a China factory is 7–10 business days. Some suppliers charge a sample fee (typically \$30–\$80), which is often refunded when you place a bulk order.

Step 5: Check Certifications and Quality Control

For socks sold in Europe or to sports brands with compliance requirements, certifications matter:

  • CE certification — required for certain categories sold in the EU
  • SGS inspection reports — independent third-party quality verification
  • AQL (Acceptable Quality Level) inspection — ask if the factory does in-line and final checks

A reliable factory will share these documents without hesitation. If a supplier deflects or can't produce certificates, treat that as a red flag.

Step 6: Confirm Lead Times and Shipping

Typical timelines for a China custom sock order:

  1. Sample production: 7–10 business days
  2. Sample approval and revision: 3–7 days
  3. Bulk production: 25–35 days
  4. Sea freight to US/Europe: 20–35 days
  5. Air freight: 5–8 days (higher cost)

Plan for 8–12 weeks total from first contact to goods in hand, especially for your first order. Experienced buyers plan seasonal stock 3–4 months in advance.

Step 7: Packaging and Private Label Options

Most China sock factories offer full private label support:

  • Custom hang tags and labels
  • Retail poly bags with barcode stickers
  • Custom header cards or box packaging
  • Inner pack configurations (pairs per polybag, pairs per carton)

Provide your packaging artwork in AI, PDF, or high-resolution PNG format. Confirm bleed and safe zone specs with your supplier before finalizing artwork.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the minimum order quantity for custom socks from China?

Most reliable manufacturers require 100–200 pairs per style. At Qiangyi Socks, our MOQ is 100 pairs per style — suitable for small brands and first-time buyers testing a new product.

How long does it take to get custom socks made in China?

Sample production takes 7–10 business days. After sample approval, bulk production takes 25–35 days. Allow 8–12 weeks total including shipping.

What certifications should a custom sock supplier have?

For European markets, CE certification is standard. SGS inspection reports are a strong independent quality signal. Always request test reports before placing bulk orders.

Can I order custom socks with my own logo and packaging?

Yes. Most China sock factories support full private label production — custom logo, colors, hang tags, retail packaging, and barcode labels are all standard options.
