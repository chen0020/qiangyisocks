Why This Sock

Built for Performance

Every construction detail is designed to keep athletes comfortable, stable, and dry through high-intensity training.

  • Terry-Loop Cushion Sole

    Honeycomb terry sole provides multi-directional shock absorption, protecting feet during running, basketball and high-intensity training.

  • Non-Slip Grip Structure

    Dedicated silicone non-slip zones keep the sock fixed inside the shoe on rapid direction changes — no bunching, no slipping.

  • Moisture-Wicking & Anti-Odor

    69.1% cotton + 25.1% polyester + 5.8% spandex. Soft, stretchy, breathable blend keeps feet dry and fresh during intense workouts.

  • Ankle Guard Support

    Reinforced ankle panel provides gentle lateral support and reduces abrasion from shoe collar during extended wear.

Common Questions

What is the minimum order quantity?

100 pairs per style for logo customization. Custom color requires 100 pairs per color. Stock samples are available at 1–5 pairs for evaluation.

How long does sampling take?

4–5 business days using stock yarn colors. 10–15 business days for custom-dyed colors. Sample fee is US$50 per style, fully refundable on your first bulk order.

What customization methods are supported?

Jacquard weave, embroidery, and screen print. We also support custom yarn color, sock height, cushion level, toe/heel color, labels, hangtags, and retail packaging.

What certifications does this product have?

CE certified (EU health, safety & environmental standards). Supplier is SGS verified with full raw material traceability documentation available on request.

Can you ship directly to Amazon FBA?

Yes. We support FBA prep including labeling, polybag, carton quantity per ASIN, and direct shipment to Amazon fulfillment centers worldwide.
