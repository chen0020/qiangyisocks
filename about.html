About Qiangyi

A sports sock manufacturer built for brands that need speed, flexibility, and consistent quality.

Who We Are

Haining Qiangyi Trading Co., Ltd. is a sports sock manufacturer based in Haining, Zhejiang — one of China's largest hosiery production clusters.

Since 2015 we have specialised in custom performance socks for sports brands, distributors, and retailers. With nine partner factories and an in-house design team, we handle everything from first sketch to export packing.

Over 91% of our production is exported. Our customers are in North America, the Middle East, Oceania, Southeast Asia, and South America. Quality is backed by an SGS onsite assessment and a 96.4% on-time dispatch record.

Capabilities

Everything you need from one supplier — design, sampling, production, inspection, and export logistics.

Design & R&D

In-house designers convert logos, sketches, or reference photos into production-ready knit files. New yarn and construction development runs continuously.

Quality Control

SGS-verified onsite assessment. Raw material traceability, in-line inspection during knitting, and final AQL sampling before packing.

Production Scale

Nine partner factories across Zhejiang. Trial runs from 100 pairs up to 100,000+ pairs per style without switching suppliers.

Customisation

Yarn (cotton, merino, bamboo, polyester, blends), colour, logo method (embroidery, jacquard, 360° print), height, size grading, and retail packaging.

Logistics

Export cartons, polybag and header card packing, Amazon FBA prep, and door-to-door air, sea, or express shipping.

Trade Assurance

Order protection on product quality and shipment timing through Alibaba Trade Assurance, plus direct contract terms for repeat buyers.
