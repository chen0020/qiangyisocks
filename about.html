About Qiangyi Socks

Your trusted partner for custom sock manufacturing since 2015.

Who We Are

Haining Qiangyi Trading Co., Ltd. is a professional socks and tights manufacturer based in Haining, Zhejiang — one of China's largest textile and hosiery production hubs.

Since our founding in 2015, we have specialized in producing high-quality custom socks for brands, wholesalers, and distributors worldwide. With 9 partner factories and an in-house R&D team, we offer end-to-end OEM/ODM services from design to delivery.

We serve customers across North America, Middle East, Oceania, Southeast Asia, and South America, with over 91% of our production exported. Our commitment to quality is verified by SGS onsite assessment and our 96.4% on-time dispatch rate.

Our Capabilities

R&D & Design

In-house R&D team continuously develops new materials, patterns, and construction techniques. We offer free design service — just send us your idea or reference image.

Quality Control

SGS-verified onsite assessment. Raw material identification & traceability, in-line inspection, and finished product inspection at every production stage.

Production Scale

9 partner factories across Zhejiang with 1,000-3,000 sqm total production area. 11-50 skilled workers, flexible capacity for orders of any size.

Customization

Full OEM/ODM: custom materials (cotton, wool, polyester, blends), colors, logos (embroidery, jacquard, print), sizes, and packaging.

Logistics & Shipping

96.4% on-time dispatch. Professional export packaging. Support for FBA prep, consolidated shipping, and direct-to-warehouse delivery.
